\section{Class-conditional warhead-control prior}\label{sec:appendix:warhead_tokens}

In addition to the unconditional covalent-fine-tuned prior described in \S\ref{sec:methods:clm}, we explored a class-conditional variant. The mol2mol tokenizer is extended with four single-token warhead-class control prefixes: \texttt{[ACRYLAMIDE]}, \texttt{[CHLOROACETAMIDE]}, \texttt{[VINYL\_SULFONAMIDE]}, \texttt{[EPOXIDE]}. The mol2mol SMILES tokenizer splits any bracketed string as one token, so these additions extend the vocabulary cleanly. We re-initialise the four new token embeddings (output-projection bias $-3$ to allow training signal to lift their probability without explosive emission early on) and fine-tune on a class-balanced corpus of Tanimoto-$\ge 0.5$ pairs across the four classes for $6$ epochs. The resulting class-conditional prior emits the requested warhead class when given the corresponding control prefix at the encoder input. We do not use this prior in the present work's experiments (which target a single acrylamide warhead). It is described here for downstream programs targeting non-acrylamide warheads, and is discussed as immediate future work in \S\ref{sec:discussion:future}.
