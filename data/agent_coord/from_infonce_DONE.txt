VERDICT: CAUSAL POSE CONTROL NOT INSTALLED. The InfoNCE contrastive term did produce a working
pose→hidden alignment gradient (pos_wins climbed from 7% random-chance floor to ~11-12%), and CFG
scaling installs measurable conditional–unconditional divergence (W grows monotonically with w).
However neither manifests as pose-clamp SPEARMAN growth. At w=1 the pose axes remain indistinguishable
from zero (same failure mode as Scheme A/B). At w=2-3 validity collapses to n<10% so the causal test
cannot even be measured. The emitted θ_BD stays ~120° regardless of Clamp D (θ=-111°) — CFG amplifies
the "canonical planar acrylamide" mode of the training distribution rather than steering the geometry.

QUALITY-ONLY WIN: InfoNCE ckpt at w=1 dominates all Scheme A/B baselines on validity, acryl retention,
planar median, and Tc-to-Mol1. Useful as a stronger CE-only generator, but NOT a pose-steerable model.

Interpretation vs Schemes A/B: adding a supervised contrastive term against pose is INSUFFICIENT because
the pose channel is 3-dim invariant (d, θ, φ) and the training data covers a narrow high-planar high-θ
manifold that dominates the loss landscape. The decoder learns "produce THE canonical acrylamide" and
gets both good CE and good InfoNCE (with p_wins slightly above chance because the correlated axes are
weakly informative). To install true steering the pose channel likely needs (a) a wider empirical
distribution over off-canonical poses, (b) a hard-negative curriculum that forces the decoder to
generate non-canonical output when off-canonical pose is clamped, or (c) an architectural change
where the pose token is fused deeper into the decoder (e.g. FiLM-conditioning each decoder layer
rather than a single memory-side token). None of these were in the Phase 3 spec.
