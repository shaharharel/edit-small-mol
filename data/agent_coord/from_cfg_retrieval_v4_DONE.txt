v4 PER-LAYER FiLM PIPELINE COMPLETE (2026-07-19).

VERDICT: FiLM ACTIVE BUT HARMFUL — only CI-significant shifts are in the
wrong direction (validity drops of -0.07 to -0.09 pp across FiLM/retrieval
combinations).  Conditioning-based approach for mol2mol appears
fundamentally dead for pocket/pose-mediated steering on ZAP70/Mol1.

FiLM TRAINING SIGNALS ARE REAL (not a null model):
  - val_loss: 17.28 -> 8.12 nat (FiLM enabled path)
  - val_loss_film_off: 17.47 -> 10.63 nat (identical decoder, FiLM disabled)
  - val_film_gap: 0.19 -> 2.51 nat (FiLM contributes 2.5 nat of teacher-
    forcing likelihood improvement over its own zero-FiLM path)

WHAT FiLM ACTUALLY DID AT SAMPLING:
  - Novelty COLLAPSED from 0.754 (baseline) to 0.370 (FiLM_on retrieval_off)
    and 0.394 (FiLM_on retrieval_on).  Model repeats a smaller set of
    canonical SMILES.  This is a mode-seeking behaviour, opposite of the
    diversity-seeking we hoped for.
  - Validity dropped 8-9 pp (CI excludes zero) — FiLM steers into
    lower-likelihood regions that produce more invalid SMILES.
  - Fukui f+ shifts marginal (+0.001, CI crosses zero at s=1 magnitude).
  - Vina cov score (--score_only) medians all identical at 119.491
    (tether-clash artefact; not discriminating; QA #6 called this out).

ARCHITECTURAL CONCLUSION:
  Both v3 (CFG-token, encoder-memory only) and v4 (per-layer FiLM +
  memory-token) fail to steer generation toward better electrophilicity or
  better docking on this target.  The training loss clearly encodes the
  pocket/pose signal (val_loss drops from 17 -> 8 in both), but at
  sampling the decoder ignores it or steers into pathological regions.

  This is now a two-architecture proof that conditioning-based steering of
  the mol2mol autoregressive decoder does NOT translate into sampling-time
  behavior change on ZAP70/Mol1.  Next steps should pivot away from this
  family:
    - Retrieval-augmented generation without any FiLM/CFG (retrieval alone
      showed the only real +0.135 validity effect in v3 at s=1.5).
    - Direct RL / DPO on Vina cov score of samples.
    - Different scaffold: DrugFlow (3D) inpaint already showed +0 validity
      hits but is a different generative family.
