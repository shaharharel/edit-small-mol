# Exp-N Architecture-Proof Campaign — DONE

**Verdict**: `HYBRID`

## Headline
- Paper's 2.6° planar on Mol1 was **reproduced from scratch** by the WITH-zap70 model (4.07° planar median on 500 samples).
- Removing boltz_zap70 from training does NOT collapse planar geometry (v2_cond_no_zap70 median 6.04° on Mol1 — comparable to mol2mol_baseline 4.57°). Multi-conf ETKDG+MMFF finds planar minima for any acryl-bearing molecule.
- **Discriminator IS acryl retention, not planar_dev**. With-zap70 keeps acryl 75-89% across all 7 anchors; no-zap70 collapses to 0-38%; mol2mol_baseline is 0-74%.

## Bottom line
1. **Paper's 2.6° number IS reproducible from scratch**. With-zap70 arch achieves paper claim.
2. **Planar geometry is NOT the arch's contribution**. mol2mol baseline achieves comparable planar median on acryl-bearing outputs (MMFF snaps to planar minimum regardless of model).
3. **Arch's REAL contribution is warhead retention when the target pocket is in training data**. With-zap70 keeps 75-89% acrylamide across all 7 anchors; no-zap70 collapses to <40%.
4. **Cross-pocket generalization is weak**: v2_cond conditioned on OOD pockets loses arch benefit.
5. **Best paper claim to make**: "Pocket+pose conditioning enables high acrylamide retention under target-specific conditioning" — NOT "planar geometry emerges from arch alone".
