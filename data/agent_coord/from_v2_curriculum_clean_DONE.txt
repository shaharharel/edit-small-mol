     or dd (opposite direction).
   - Fix in v1_clean: --require_both_geoms 1 (strict AND). Curriculum pass_ALL:
     23.2% -> 100.0% on train, 19.1% -> 100.0% on val. Zero negative deltas.
   - Curriculum train pairs: 6340 -> 2248 (-64.5%). Crystal-spliced pairs
     (908 train, 228 val) copied verbatim from v1_dirty for apples-to-apples.
   - Same pocket split (seed=0, 20% val), verified 0 train/val overlap.

2. Training (Phase C, 10k steps on A100):
   - Warm-start: models/m1a_v2.ckpt (same as v1_dirty).
   - v1_clean final KL_A_vs_B=0.397, KL_A_vs_C=0.392, mean-last-3=0.435/0.437.
   - v1_dirty final KL_A_vs_B=0.438, KL_A_vs_C=0.472, mean-last-3=0.423/0.440.
   - Training-curve KL is essentially a tie (v1_clean +1% on mean-last-3).
   - Best val 24.04 @ step 1000 (early convergence on smaller dataset).

3. Steering diagnostic (Phase D — 100 mols per cohort, 4 cohorts):
   - Sample validity: 40-57% (avg 49%) — well below v1_dirty's 62%.
     CAVEAT: 64.5% training-pair cut caused earlier decoder overfitting; the
     10k-step budget was too long for the smaller dataset. Retrain suggestion:
     500-2000 steps for v1_clean, or use best.chkpt @ step 1000 next time.
   - Acrylamide retention on VALID subset: 68-93% (comparable to v1_dirty).
   - 162 Boltz cofolds harvested across 4 cohorts (theta_90:40, theta_105:50,
     theta_130:48, null_pose:24).

4. Phase E head-to-head:
   - Boltz-observed |median(theta_130 - theta_90)|: v1_clean=10.1°, v1_dirty=1.6°
     -> v1_clean shows 6x stronger steering signal.
   - KS-test (theta_90 vs theta_130): v1_clean p=0.10 (borderline),
     v1_dirty p=0.64 (null-consistent).
   - KS-test (theta_90 vs null_pose): v1_clean p=0.048 (significant),
     v1_dirty p=0.37 (null-consistent) -> v1_clean IS listening to pose.
   - Wasserstein distances larger for v1_clean on every pairwise contrast.
   - v1_dirty theta_90 cohort produces median 150.1 deg (no response to
     theta_target=90). v1_clean theta_90 cohort produces median 136.1 deg
     (a genuine downward shift toward the target, 14 deg below v1_dirty).

5. Engineering notes / gotchas:

     (nohup shell dropped conda env). Fixed by wrapping in bash -lc with
     conda activate quris.

     via python subprocess PATH search. Fixed by patching
     experiments/boltz_verdict_driver.py to use absolute BOLTZ_BIN
     env var (defaults to conda-env boltz).
   - Third attempt (8 parallel workers) hit CUDA OOM cascade on A100 40GB
     after ~62 cofolds. Fixed by running 4 cohorts SERIALLY with
     max_workers=2 each -> 2 concurrent boltz predicts (safe).
   - Final v1_dirty cofold_track had only theta_90 -- other 3 CSVs were

     Copied the 3 missing dirty CSVs from campaign track_A files.
