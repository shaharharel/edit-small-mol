## HEADLINE
**v2_cond_denoise is the new SMILES winner** on 7-anchor broad eval: 75.5% avg warhead-match, wins 4/7 anchors. Denoise+DAP composition slightly worse (73.1%). Pure DAP-RL matches Exp-N base (72.3% vs 72.0%). Denoise ALONE is the best SMILES method tonight.

**DrugFlow (3D) wins on Mol1 metrics** (100% validity, 0.53° planar) but only tested on 1 anchor + has near-zero anchor conservation (Murcko 0.07 — de novo design, not lead-opt).

**Attribution refined**:
- Architecture (pocket+pose): +51pp warhead-match over no-zap70 (72% vs 20%)
- Denoise pair training: **+4pp warhead-match** over base autoencoder (76% vs 72%) — the ONLY intervention that actually improves the strongest baseline
- DAP RL: no measurable warhead gain over base (+0.3pp), preserves geometry
- Denoise + DAP composition: NOT additive — actually slightly regresses (73% vs 76% denoise-alone)
