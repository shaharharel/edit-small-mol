Column Reference
ID (compound_id)Stable compound identifier when assigned; otherwise the dataset row_id (greyed). Use to cross-reference across reports.
pIC50 (mean ± std)Predicted ZAP70 pIC50 — mean ± std across 3 FiLMDelta ensemble seeds, each anchor-averaged over 280 ZAP70 training mols. Higher = more potent. ± shows model disagreement.
LLELipophilic Ligand Efficiency = pIC50 − LogP (Leeson & Springthorpe 2007). Discriminates real potency from lipophilicity-driven artifacts. Target ≥ 5 for kinase leads.
LELigand Efficiency = 1.4 · pIC50 / n_heavy_atoms (kcal/mol per heavy atom; Hopkins 2004). Size-corrected potency. Target ≥ 0.3 for fragment/lead.
strain (capped)Ligand internal conformational strain in kcal/mol from capped MM-GBSA methodology. Replaces broken ligand_strain_kcalmol. Lower = lower internal energy cost; > 5 kcal/mol is concerning.
log k2 (GSH)Predicted log k2 reaction rate vs glutathione — xTB-derived warhead intrinsic reactivity. Higher = more reactive electrophile.
ΔG_recogNon-covalent recognition ΔG in kcal/mol (K_I proxy for the pre-covalent step) from capped MM-GBSA. Replaces broken dG_bind_kcalmol. More negative = stronger recognition.
Vina (kcal/mol)AutoDock Vina rescore on the Boltz cofold pose. Knowledge-based docking score, orthogonal to MM-GBSA force-field physics — sanity-check ΔG_recog. More negative = better.
d_SG (Å)Warhead Cβ to Cys346 Sγ distance in the cofold pose. Canonical Michael-addition ≈ 1.8 Å; > 2.5 Å = warhead not engaged. — = warhead atom undetected.
pKa Cys346Predicted Cys346 thiol pKa (PROPKA3) in the cofold pocket. Lower pKa = more thiolate at physiological pH = activated nucleophile.
ligand iptmBoltz-2 ligand-specific interface ipTM — most discriminating Boltz confidence for ligand-pocket interface. Preferred over the whole-complex iptm.
Δ vs Mol1pIC50candidate − pIC50Mol 1. Positive = predicted more potent than Mol 1 (~6.77).
direct δ from Mol1Direct FiLMDelta(Mol 1, candidate) prediction averaged across 3 seeds. Single-anchor prediction (Mol 1 only), noisier than the anchor-mean Δ but more interpretable.
wins / 280Number of the 280 ZAP70 training anchors where the candidate is predicted to be MORE potent (i.e., FiLMDelta predicts Δ > 0). Range 0–280. Higher = predicted to beat more of the training set.
wins ≥ 7Same as wins/280 but RESTRICTED to high-potency training anchors only (those with measured pIC50 ≥ 7.0; ~55 of the 280). Count of those the candidate is predicted to beat. The harder/cleaner bar — predicted to outperform actually potent compounds.
SASErtl-Schuffenhauer Synthetic Accessibility Score. 1 = trivial to make, 10 = essentially impossible. Most drug-like molecules are 2–4.
MWMolecular weight (Daltons). Drug-like < 500. We allowed up to ~700 in this library.
Tc → Mol1Morgan fingerprint (radius=2, 2048 bits) Tanimoto similarity to Mol 1. 0 = unrelated, 1 = identical. ≥ 0.4 = clear analog.
max Tc → trainMaximum Tanimoto to the closest of the 280 ZAP70 training mols. ≥ 0.5 = within model's training distribution. < 0.3 = OOD extrapolation regime where FiLMDelta MAE ≈ 0.86.
shape Tc3D shape Tanimoto vs Mol 1 — RDKit O3A-aligned Gaussian-volume similarity over a 4-conformer ensemble (best alignment kept). 1 = identical 3D shape; 0 = unrelated. > 0.7 = strong 3D footprint preservation. Computed only for top-100 per method.
ESP-SimEspsim electrostatic Tanimoto vs Mol 1 — combined shape + Gasteiger-charge electrostatic similarity after alignment. −1 to +1; positive = matching polar regions. Catches differences ESP-blind shape Tc misses. Top-100 per method only.
warhead Δ°MIN angle (degrees) between Mol 1's C=C–C(=O)–N warhead vector and the candidate's, over a conformer ensemble after MCS-based 3D alignment. 0° = warhead points the same way (good covalent reach to catalytic Cys); > 90° = pointing away. Top-100 per method only.
PAINSPan-Assay Interference Compounds alerts — count of substructures known to cause false positives in screening. 0 = clean; ≥ 1 = potential issue.

Top 20 — Combined Boltz × FiLMDelta

Ranked by z(pIC50_FiLMDelta) − z(mPAE_Boltz) + 0.5·z(iptm_Boltz) over the 997 cofolded MW<400 candidates.

Each row: pIC50 says how potent FiLMDelta predicts the molecule. Boltz says how confident the cofolded binding pose is. The "Cofold Pose Quality" columns report Boltz-2 pose geometry — dSG ≈ 1.8 Å is the canonical Michael-addition warhead-Cys distance. These are geometric/contact signals, not binding free energies. Tier 4 — Covalent-Aware Generation

New-generation covalent-aware sequence methods. Unlike Tier 3 v2/v3 (warhead enforced by post-hoc SMARTS gate), these methods learn a covalent-mol distribution from the CovInDB 2.0 corpus. Both share the same base architecture (Mol2Mol transformer) but differ in how the warhead constraint is taught.

EXP2 — Mol2Mol Covalent FT (no RL)

Mol2Mol transformer prior fine-tuned via REINVENT4 TL on 855 CovInDB covalent SMILES. No RL, no token prefix. Outputs naturally retain acrylamide ~90% of the time. Best Cβ-SASA (4.86 Å²) among all cohorts — the compact CovInDB geometry transfers cleanly to pocket fit.

EXP6 — Mol2Mol Warhead-Class Vocab Tokens + FT

Mol2Mol with extended vocabulary (`[ACRYLAMIDE]`, `[CHLOROACETAMIDE]`, `[VINYL_SULFONAMIDE]`, `[EPOXIDE]`) + FT on 5,488 CovInDB pairs prefixed by the appropriate token. Best diversity (422 scaffolds / 500 mols) and best AD-CovDock Vina score (126.6 kcal/mol). The vocab tokens give explicit warhead-class control.
