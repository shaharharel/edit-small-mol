# CovalentFormer v7 label corpus — generations

Generated by `dataset_manifest.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every label record carries a `label_generation` field. The generations obey
**different sentence rules** and must not be mixed in training without a
deliberate decision: a model trained across g1 and g2 together learns to emit
invented precision some fraction of the time, and no metric computed afterwards
can separate the two sources.

Generation is assigned by batch number. `make_full_batches.py` seeds its shuffle
with a fixed 7, so batch N holds the same 100 pairs across every rebuild and a
batch number is a durable identity.

## One record per pair

Every label file holds exactly one record per pair, so a plain load of
`label_out/batch_*.json` is correct and cannot double-count.

Fifty g1 pairs were re-adjudicated after two identity-disclosure flags were added
to the packet (`COVALENCY MISMATCH IS UNRELIABLE HERE`, `HETERO PARTNER
RENUMBERED`). Those fixes were MERGED BACK into batches 0001-0010 rather than kept
as separate files. Each carries `readjudicated_from`, `readjudicated_reason`,
`verdict_changed`, and the superseded reading under `previous_adjudication`, so
nothing was discarded and the record explains its own history. 6 of the 50
verdicts changed and 0 of the 50 sentences came back identical -- including two
that had asserted a covalent-bond loss the geometry contradicts. The original
files are at `label_out/archive_readjudicated/`, outside the glob.

## What changed between g1 and g2, and why

g1 sentences report the analogue crystal and cite its measured distances. At
inference the model has no analogue structure and cannot measure anything, so
those targets teach it to invent precision. g2 splits the sentence into an
input-side MEASURED anchor and an analogue-side QUALITATIVE consequence:
direction and kind are predictable from the pocket, magnitude is not.

Measured on g1 against the g2 rules: 8.2% cite no analogue distance, 23.0% use no
analogue-provenance phrasing, 3.1% satisfy all six hard rules. So g1 is
effectively 100% one style. Converting it is a chair rewrite of 1,715 usable
sentences, not a re-label.

## What g3 adds

The 8 A pocket envelope. g2 forbids naming a residue absent from the input table,
but that table lists only each atom NEAREST protein atom, so on ~24% of pairs
there is no legitimate residue to name for where a new group lands. Measured on 70
pairs, an 8 A envelope recovers 94% of those mentions (6 A: 75%, 10 A: 96%).
g2 labels are therefore deliberately vaguer in their consequence clause than g3
labels will be, and that difference is a property of the evidence, not of the
labellers.
