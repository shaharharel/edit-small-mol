Independent chemistry review of crystallographic rationale labels

This packet contains ten sentences with their full evidence: six reproducibly random cases from the first 1,100 finalized labels, and four deliberately selected stress cases. It is a qualitative spot check, not a representative population error-rate estimate. Original verdicts and adjudication reasons are withheld until your scores are recorded.

Please read PIPELINE.md and briefs/REVIEWER.md for the training objective, then evidence_and_labels.txt. Use scorecard.csv to record judgments. Evaluate the sentence, not its fluency or whether it resembles an example. Distinguish a flawed sentence from a pair whose evidence cannot support a positive training example.

For each case:
1. Do both SMILES support the named chemical edit, ring size, substituent, and stereochemical or multi-site description?
2. Is the measured anchor genuinely input-side, correctly assigned to its chemical group, and supported by reliable geometry and typing?
3. Does the qualitative pocket consequence follow from the edit and evidence, without inventing interactions or treating an envelope band as a contact?
4. Are retained/lost hydrogen bonds, covalent bonds, and metal interactions supported despite atom-mapping, numbering, uncertainty, symmetry, and chain caveats?
5. Would this teach a model useful prospective reasoning, or merely a generic claim that the group redistributes?

Rationale usefulness: 1 = misleading or no usable pocket rationale; 2 = thin or largely generic; 3 = defensible but limited; 4 = useful and specific; 5 = clear, chemically sound, well grounded rationale.

For any concern, identify the relevant atom/group, residue, table row or caveat. A suggested repair should be one sentence of at most 35 words, with only input-side measurements and a qualitative consequence. Keep uncertainty explicit. Do not force repairs merely to satisfy a lexical checker. No potency evidence is supplied.

The AI review, if present, is a separate model opinion, not a human chemist endorsement. For an independent human assessment, do not read ai_review.json or sample_key.json until completing the scorecard.
